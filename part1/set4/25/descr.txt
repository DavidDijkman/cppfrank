Explaination of 'encapsulation' in the context of classes:
Encapsulation means that the inside of the class is not visible to the
outside, a class functions on its own and cannot be maniuplated by the
 outside. The outside only communicates with the class through an interface. 

 Explaination of 'data hiding' in the context of classes:
 'data hiding' is the technique used to realize encapsulation. Data members of
 the class are declared private and inaccessible from outside the class. 
 Data member's access is only allowed through member functions.

Why are these concepts important to consider when building functions:
As all data changes go through member functions, it is guaranteed that data 
always remains in a valid state. Also, users only need to understand the 
interface, improving the simplicity of use.
